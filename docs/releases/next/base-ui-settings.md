### Improved

- Settings opens as a dialog over the window: ⌘, or the sidebar's gear opens it, the pages list sits in a column on the left with the search on top, and the page you pick shows on the right. The Settings tab and the sidebar's list of pages are gone on the desktop; the iPad keeps its sheet.
- The dialog's rows are new: the label with a small description under it, the value at its end - a pill to pick from, an opaque switch to turn on, a tick box to check. The search inside it jumps to the row it finds.
- Editing, Shortcuts, Images, Export, Privacy and the Textus AI pages (providers, memory and skills, writing assist, external tools) join General and Appearance in the dialog's new shells: their rows read and write the store; the capture rows, the storage and file rows, the providers' panels and the folds keep their sections for now.
- The window wears the Slate skin: a cool chrome around a near-white page, glass cards with an 18px radius and a soft shadow, and selected rows that use a quiet fill, an accent icon and a 600 name. The desktop settings dialog is 980×640, with a 260px page column, a 44px glass top bar, no page title, 56/72px rows and hairlines that start at the text and stop 16px short of the card.
- The desktop view column is 260px and the text column about 700px at 1440; the right page edge is 340px. The notes in the view column - the current delivery and the recent files - use the same bare rows as the file tree instead of white buttons or a separate card.
- The phone's Today, library and settings share the same glass cards, bare line icons, inset hairlines, 48×28 switches and selected-file treatment; the tablet keeps the desktop structure at a narrower width.
- The conversation names the asset library's tools - searching, reading a card, drafting, proposing a patch, asking for confirmation and recording a use - instead of their internal names.

### Fixed

- The recent files at the top of the desktop file view no longer draw as native browser buttons, and the current delivery is no longer a lone white card among bare rows.
- Settings cards draw one hairline in the right place: the last row keeps the line above it, legacy pages no longer double it, and the card's top and bottom insets match its rows.
- Sync uses the same card rows, rounded fields and drawn actions as the rest of Settings; Export no longer repeats its page name in the content.
- The Create buttons under Extensions › Themes, Plugins and Skills open Create inside the Settings dialog, not a tab behind it.
- Collecting a thought on the phone opens the note it just wrote, and that note can be opened again from Today.
- Settings no longer puts its search field under the column divider; the Export and External tools pages have their icons, and the last page scrolls above the fade.
- The fixed-shortcut list sits in its card with the same 20px text start and inset hairlines as the rows above it.
- The window's hamburger menu is gone on the desktop: a search button opens the command palette, which lists every menu item, and the optional menu bar still shows the full menus.
- The interface icons are a step larger on the desktop, phone and file tree.
- The phone keeps a row's control on the right when its description wraps, so its selects and switches keep the desktop's shape.
- The Create tab fills its page again, with its empty box in the middle instead of at the top.
