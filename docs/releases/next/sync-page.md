### Improved

- Settings › Sync is one page: the current connection sits under the vault's status, and switching, a new or edited connection and the local Hub open in place instead of on pages of their own. Only the fields a service needs show at first; encryption and the rest wait under Advanced.
- An S3 connection names its service — standard S3, Bitiful, Alibaba Cloud OSS or Qiniu Kodo — in the form, the connection summary, the switch list and the library list. The keys are labelled Access Key (AK) and Secret Key (SK), and the form's question mark says where to find them.
- A switch says what it guarantees before a choice: this library only, the old cloud's files stay, preview first. Each library in the list shows its own last sync, and times follow the interface language.
- Saving a new connection or a switch ends in a preview of what the next sync would do.
