### Improved

- A single island of documents is the page itself now: no floating card around it - the paper runs to the window's edges, with the tabs still in the window bar or on their own row. Split the window and the islands float again, so you can tell them apart; Zen is unchanged.
- The default palette takes the design boards' values: a slightly brighter paper (#fbf8f2), a deeper ink (#241f19), a more ember accent (#b0531f), and their secondary grey, hairline and code sheet with them - the whole window follows, dark keeps its night sheet.
